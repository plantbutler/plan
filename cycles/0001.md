## Notes from the betting table

**Software first, hardware after.** Jacopo cannot touch the hardware for at least the first two
weeks of the cycle, and none of the software pitches ever depended on the hardware ones — only
the reverse. So the four bets are the org setup and the three pitches that put a reading on the
NAS and a command on the wire. The bench rig and everything downstream of it wait for cycle 2.

**What can slip.** "Org, repos, decisions" is the committed chore. "Readings land on the NAS" and
"Readings up the wire" are the two bets that can fail, for the two reasons the plan exists to
find out early: whether a container stands up on the Synology, and whether the idle board still
talks over WiFiS3. "Command hand-off" is the stretch: it is software only and testable against a
fake device, so it is the first thing to cut if the other two need the room.

**2026-08-30, the day before the table.** The org chore is most of the way done before the cycle
starts: org, repos, transfer, umbrella, decisions, an `AGENTS.md` in every repo, the app password
revoked. Left: order the bench parts. The plan's own tool
got fixed on the way — openproj 0.43.0 serves from a pip install and warns on a checkout.

**Two people, 0.2 and 0.4.** Six build weeks buy Jacopo 1.2 person-weeks and Claude 2.4; the
four pitches add up to 2.25 and sit on separate people — Jacopo on the org and the board, Claude on
the two backend pitches — so on the derived schedule the last of them ends on 2026-10-05, inside
the build. Claude's rate is not independent of Jacopo's, so treat its load bar as optimistic.

**2026-09-03, the bench arrived early.** The table bet software-first because Jacopo could not
touch the hardware for the first two weeks; that premise expired after four days. Everything is now
on the bench — pump, brick, relay, flow meter, reservoir, float — so the hardware bets are no longer
blocked and the goal's closing clause, "while the bench is off-limits", no longer describes the
cycle. The goal is left as it was bet.

The one unstarted bet, "Readings up the wire", is shelved and replaced in the same slot by "Bench
sketch": the same reporting work plus the nine bring-up commands and the safety measures that stand
in for the interlock DECISIONS #10 removed. 0.75 person-weeks becomes 1.5, on Claude with Jacopo
reviewing and running bring-up 0-7d. Three follow-ups were shaped out of it rather than into it —
"The watchdog that survives a stopped clock", "Trust the tank" and "I refilled the tank".

Ten pitches have landed in this cycle against four bet, and Claude's availability was corrected
from a guessed 0.4 to the 13.0 the record implies. Both are the same observation: the plan
underestimated how much would get built, not how fast the bench would arrive.
