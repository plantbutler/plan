## Notes from the betting table

**Software first, hardware after.** Jacopo cannot touch the hardware for at least the first two
weeks of the cycle, and none of the software pitches ever depended on the hardware ones — only
the reverse. So the four bets are the org setup and the three pitches that put a reading on the
NAS and a command on the wire. The bench rig and everything downstream of it wait for cycle 2.

**What can slip.** "Org, repos, decisions" is the committed chore. "Readings land on the NAS" and
"Readings up the wire" are the two bets that can fail, for the two reasons the plan exists to
find out early: whether a container stands up on the Synology, and whether the idle board still
talks over WiFiS3. "Command hand-off" is the stretch: it is software only and testable against a
fake device, so it is the first thing to cut if the other two need the room.
