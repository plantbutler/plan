Inspiration: <https://getplanta.com>. Each pot carries pot size, soil type, plant type and plant
size — descriptive columns in the pots table, edited from "Manage the garden". If this ever grows
toward species-driven care profiles (what Planta actually sells), it collides with the "no species
database / no per-species profiles" no-gos and must become its own pitch; the four fields cost
nothing now and make the app worth looking at.
