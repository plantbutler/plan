## Problem
"Trust the tank" judged the float against a clock: a tap of "refilled" that the float did not
move across within three minutes was a stuck float, and the rules went dry. That presumed the
tap flipped the float, which a top-up of a not-yet-empty tank never does — Jacopo tops up daily —
so the first tap after a top-up stopped the automatic watering until the tank actually ran empty.

## Appetite
One day. It became two, in five review rounds, because the wire is coarse and every rule we wrote
for one ordering broke another (the pour arriving after the tap, a retried report, a contra out of
an untapped refill).

## Solution
Jacopo's rule: the meter counts what leaves the tank, so the tank's size is measured, not
guessed. Every tap means "full to the top"; the water pumped between a tap and the float going
empty is one sample; the median of the last five is the size, armed after two. A float still
saying full once more than the size has been pumped since the tap is presumed stuck: the rules go
dry, the phone is paged, the next tap is the clear. A float still saying empty in a reading three
minutes after a tap made while it read empty is presumed dead, or the board's own float check
tripped: a page naming both. Every measured run is announced; one more than a quarter off is a
warning. The app shows the size, the counter, a learning hint and OVER, and says what the tap
means. Nothing changes on the board.

The rabbit hole the last pitch named — "guessing tank volume from flow totals" — is this pitch,
and it was right to name it: the counter needed an origin (a tap, or the float's rise after a
drop that followed the tap), the word had to hold for two reports, a contra-forced 0 had to be
remembered as no drop, and a retried report had to be dropped before the float logic.
Design: `backend/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-06-tank-size-design.md`, D1–D14.

Two things found on the way and fixed here because the pages send a person down the same steps:
the latch instructions said `clear contra` for a board latched dry by a reset (it needs `dry
off`), and `err=i2c` was refused by the token regex, so one I2C refusal made every later report a
400 until reboot.

## Rabbit holes
- Telling a flap-forced 0 from an empty tank: the wire cannot; firmware#4 asks for a channel.
- A second reset mid-dose before any dose has ended never latches: backend#27.

## No-gos
No firmware change. No per-tank UI: the tap is the whole interface.
