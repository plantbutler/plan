## Problem
The bench rig has to be wired before anything can be measured, and nothing said which board pin
goes where, how the pump is switched, or how the float keeps it off. The mux and the IO expander
were a plan in a chat, not a drawing.

## Appetite
Half a person-week.

## Solution
Four drawings and a pin map for one manifold with five pots: the sensors through a CD74HC4067
behind a PCF8575 so more pots and manifolds bolt on, the servo and its two halls, the flow meter,
and the pump on a MOSFET driver whose gate rises only when the firmware asserts PUMP_EN and the
float hall sees its magnet. One source of truth (`nets.py`); the README and the drawings are
generated from it. Every part that was ordered but not yet in hand is flagged "verify".

## Rabbit holes
- KiCad and a PCB: after the bench has fixed the part choices.
- The bench firmware's serial commands: named in the README, written under "Pump on command".

## No-gos
No PCB, no enclosure, no second manifold wired yet.
