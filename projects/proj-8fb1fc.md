No Android toolchain, no app. The moment the project exists for — see a pot's curve from the sofa
and tap to water it — needs a phone in the loop.

## Appetite
Cycles 4-6, one pitch each, each ending with something on the phone.

## Solution
Hello pots, then water-now and a chart, then managing the garden.

## No-gos
No Play Store, no login, no offline mode, no widgets in v1.
