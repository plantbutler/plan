## Problem
The board reads A0-A3 onto two screens and cycles the manifold at boot; it has never posted a
reading over WiFiS3 in earnest and never driven a pump.

## Appetite
Cycles 1-3: readings first (software only), pump, homing and interlocks once the bench exists.

## Solution
Four pitches, in order: readings up the wire, pump on command, a manifold that knows where it is,
and the interlocks that make unattended automation safe to switch on.

## No-gos
No OTA, no offline buffering, no scheduler in firmware, no mechanism redesign under this milestone.
