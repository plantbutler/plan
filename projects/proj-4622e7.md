## Problem
The manifold exists only physically, nothing has ever pumped water under software control, and the
sensors are uncoated.

## Appetite
Cycles 2-3, as soon as the bench is available again.

## Solution
A bench rig with a wiring diagram and BOM, sealed sensor stakes, and the manifold and mounts in
OpenSCAD once the homing pitch says the mechanism is worth modelling.

## No-gos
No PCB, no flow meter, no enclosure, no redesign until the homing pitch asks for one.
